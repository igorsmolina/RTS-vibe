# Prompts dos novos times

Cada bloco é um prompt completo e independente para uma IA de imagem sem contexto do jogo. Copie um bloco por vez.

- **Azul:** inspirado em veículos franceses reais.
- **Roxo:** país fictício, mistura de Alemanha e Ásia (design original).

Depois de gerar, o jogo usa os sprites reduzidos para 128 × 128.

## Time azul (França)

### Azul 1: Tanque leve

```text
Create ONE game sprite: a single military vehicle for a top-down 2D strategy game. View: strictly orthographic, directly from above (90 degrees), no perspective, no isometric or side view. The vehicle's front points straight UP toward the top edge of the image. One vehicle only, centered, filling about 85% of a square 1024x1024 canvas with a small empty margin; nothing cropped. Style: hand-painted semi-realistic military illustration, crisp dark outlines, moderate shading, soft light from the upper left, bold simple shapes readable when shrunk to 50x50 pixels; not pixel art, not a photo. Background: fully transparent PNG with real alpha channel; no ground, no cast shadow, no checkerboard, no border. No text, numbers, flags, insignia, logos, watermark or people.
Subject: a fast light wheeled reconnaissance tank inspired by the French AMX-10 RC: six large rubber wheels (three per side), low wide flat hull, small angular turret placed slightly forward, one long 105mm gun pointing straight forward. No tracks.
Paint: deep navy blue and slate gray two-tone, pale gray accents, black tires/tracks, dark steel details. No olive green, no red.
```
